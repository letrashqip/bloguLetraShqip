# Read Aloud: Listen to Books in Albanian

Reading does not always happen in front of a quiet screen. We may be travelling, preparing dinner, or simply giving our eyes a rest. LetraShqip’s **Read Aloud** feature makes your library listenable while keeping Albanian at the centre of the experience.

For blog articles and compatible books, select “Listen to article”. The platform uses the Drita voice, prepared for Albanian sounds, names, and punctuation. Text is processed on your device and the model is cached locally after its first download, making later listening sessions faster.

## A rhythm for everyday life

Listening is not meant to replace reading. It creates another way to approach a book. Read a chapter on screen and continue later by voice, listen to an introduction before committing to a full work, or revisit a difficult passage.

Read Aloud is especially useful for learners connecting written language with pronunciation, readers using a phone while travelling, and anyone who wants to keep Albanian close during a busy day.

## Start simply

Sign in, open an article or book, and choose the listening option when it is available. You can stop at any time and return to the text. The goal is simple: more time with the ideas and stories that matter to you.
