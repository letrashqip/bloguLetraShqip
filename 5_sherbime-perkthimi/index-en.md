## What we translate

Our experienced team works on texts that need different rules, not the same care on every page.

- **Books and publications** — prose, poetry, essays, monographs, texts that come out as a book with a publisher
- **Academic texts** — papers, articles, theses, scientific chapters, with terminology that stays the same through the whole text
- **Literary texts** — short stories, novels, poetry, drama, where the rhythm and the voice of the author are part of the work
- **Technical texts** — instructions, catalogs, manuals, documentation, text for websites and for promotional material

For every type of text the way of working is chosen too. A poem needs solutions that flow, a technical text needs the same term on every page.

## Our principles

### Accuracy

Numbers, names, terms and facts do not change. When a passage in the original can be read in two ways, it is marked and asked about: what the author meant is not decided in silence.

### High quality

The text is translated, then read as a text in that language — no pull from the source language, no heavy sentences, no wording that sounds translated. Every delivery goes through editing and proofreading before it reaches you.

## How the work goes

1. **Send us the text** — or a description of it, with the source language, the target language and the date you need
2. **Get the offer** — the price and the deadline before the work starts
3. **Translation** — by a translator who works with that type of text
4. **Editing and proofreading** — a full read in the target language
5. **Delivery** — the text in the format you need, inside the deadline
