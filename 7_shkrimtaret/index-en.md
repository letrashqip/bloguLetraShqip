# Writers: publish your book and take 70% of every sale

Do you have a manuscript ready, or a story that has been waiting for a while to be shared with the world? LetraShqip accepts manuscripts from new writers and from experienced authors — publishing through the platform, with 70% of the revenue from every sale going to the author.

There is no long publishing process, no waiting for months and no second number that follows your work in silence. Your book enters the catalog, readers find it and you watch the earnings yourself, online.

## What we offer

### 70% of the revenue is yours

From every book sold, the larger part is yours. Your work and your talent deserve to be paid properly.

### Publishing in a few steps

You do not need to go through long and complicated processes. You send the manuscript from the author panel, with the title, the genre and the price you suggest yourself.

### Your audience is waiting

Your books are available to the readers who use letrashqip.com and the Android app, including reading with voice — a new way to reach an audience, besides the printed pages.

### A public page for the author

Your public profile keeps your biography, your pen name and your links, so the reader finds not only the book but also the author.

### Earnings you can see yourself

In the author panel you have the total earned, the report per book and the export of the royalty statement, when you need it for documents.

## Who can apply

- New writers publishing for the first time
- Experienced authors looking for a more flexible platform
- Any author who writes in Albanian and has a manuscript ready or in progress
- Authors who want their book translated, with the translation workspace inside the panel

## How the process works

![Five steps, from the manuscript to the sale](images/02-procesi.webp)

1. **Register** as an author at letrashqip.com/author/register
2. **Sign the author agreement**, once, before your first submission
3. **Submit the book** — the manuscript, the title, the genre, the description and the price you suggest
4. **Review** — our team reads the work and the book keeps the status "in review"
5. **Publishing and earnings** — the book enters the catalog and you take 70% of every sale

At any moment you can move the book back to draft to edit it and publish it again when it is ready. A price change is requested from the panel, not by email.

## Earnings and payouts

- **Total earned** and the earning per book, split by period
- **Royalty statement** that you export when you need it
- **Electronic payouts** — connect your account for automatic payments or receive the payment by bank transfer
- **Payout request** for confirmed balances, with a minimum of 25 dollars

## What changes compared to traditional publishing

- **You take the larger part**, not the publishing house
- **You suggest the price** and you request the change from the panel
- **You follow the status of the book yourself**: draft, in review, published
- **Your data does not stay in an email** — you see the sales and the earnings whenever you want

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I pay to publish?** No. The platform takes its part from the sales, out of the revenue of the books sold.

**Do I have to be an experienced author?** No. We also accept authors publishing for the first time.

**How long does the review take?** The time depends on the queue and on the type of manuscript. You follow the status of the book from the panel.

**Can I pull a book back from publishing?** Yes. The book moves back to draft from the panel, and in draft you edit it and publish it again.

**Can I have a translated book?** Yes. The author panel also keeps the translation projects, split by book. See our [translation services](https://letrashqip.com/blog/en/sherbime-perkthimi).

**How do I start?** Register at letrashqip.com/author/register, sign the agreement and submit your first manuscript.
